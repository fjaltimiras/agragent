How well can yield actually be predicted?
Yield predictability depends on spatial scale. These are measured results on three public datasets, all using Extra Trees regression. They are not this field's estimate: they indicate how much confidence an estimate at each scale deserves.
The practical consequence: satellite and weather data alone do not resolve within-field yield variation. At that scale, ground observations such as inflorescence counts carry the signal.
